About Pono Law Group

Experienced, ethical, and deeply committed to Hawaii's families and businesses.

Our Story

Righteous Counsel, Rooted in Hawaii

Pono Law Group was founded in 2011 by Keahi Nakamura and Lena Yoshida, two Honolulu-raised attorneys who met at the University of Hawaii William S. Richardson School of Law and shared a vision of building a law firm that reflected Hawaiian values — particularly the concept of pono, meaning righteousness, balance, and justice.

Both attorneys had worked at larger mainland-affiliated firms and witnessed how generic, one-size-fits-all legal approaches often failed Hawaii's unique community. Hawaii has distinct laws, customs, and cultural considerations — particularly around real estate (land court vs. regular system), family dynamics (multigenerational households, adoption customs), and the state's complex relationship with federal immigration law and indigenous rights.

Today, Pono Law Group employs four attorneys and serves hundreds of Hawaii families and businesses each year. Our Fort Street Mall location in downtown Honolulu is accessible by TheBus and close to the state courts we appear in regularly.
